Lecturer and team leader at the Euler Institute, Università della Svizzera italiana (USI), researcher at UniDistance Suisse, and Adjunct Professor at the University of Victoria, Canada. I work on numerical methods and open-source software for contact, fluid–structure interaction and coupled problems, with a focus on high-performance computing.

Research

Research statement

Coupling non-matching discretizations

Parallel variational transfer between arbitrarily distributed meshes, combining geometric search, load balancing and assembly of L2-projections; dual Lagrange multiplier methods for fractured porous media and rock contact.

SIAM J. Sci. Comput. 2016 · J. Comput. Phys. 2021 · Int. J. Rock Mech. 2020

Fluid–structure–contact interaction

Immersed methods coupling fluids with hyperelastic structures in contact, and their local conservation properties. Applications to bioprosthetic aortic valves and, with an industrial partner, liquid diaphragm pumps.

J. Comput. Phys. 2019 · EP Europace 2021 · BIT 2025 · DD XXVIII 2026

Solvers & high-performance computing

Matrix-free multigrid and augmented Lagrangian methods for contact problems on GPUs, in mixed precision. Related work on polarized radiative transfer and distributed sorting with MPI collectives.

J. Comput. Phys. 2023 · J. Parallel Distrib. Comput. 2024 · submitted 2026

Uncertainty quantification

Space-time multilevel Monte Carlo for cardiac electrophysiology, and samplers of Gaussian random fields via SPDEs on embedded meshes, with Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory.

J. Comput. Phys. 2021 · Numer. Linear Algebra Appl. 2018

Collaborations LLNL · Portland State · Stanford · ETH Zürich · University of Bern · University of Turin · IRSOL · HSLU · KNF Flodos AG · CSCS
